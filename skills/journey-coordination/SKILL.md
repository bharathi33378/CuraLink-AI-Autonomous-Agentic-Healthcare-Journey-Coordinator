# Healthcare Journey Coordination Skill

## Purpose
Coordinates the end-to-end logistics of a doctor visit for elderly patients through an autonomous 8-step execution pipeline.

## Capabilities
1. **Context Retrieval**: Reads authorized patient identity, primary conditions, and medications.
2. **Specialist Selection**: Identifies assigned cardiologist or alternative physicians.
3. **Availability & Fee Check**: Queries clinic suite and tele-consult slots.
4. **Clinical Summary Synthesis**: Prepares verified doctor brief for patient review.
5. **Transit Dispatch**: Arranges wheelchair-accessible senior cab or family ride.
6. **Reminders & Alarms**: Configures departure alarms and pill intake alerts.
7. **Caregiver Alerts**: Dispatches SMS updates to designated family members.
