# EXPLAINABILITY

## Decision Reasoning and How It Decides
CuraLink AI evaluates each patient interaction using a deterministic clinical triage and eight-step journey coordination engine. When a patient speaks or submits a request, the reasoning model first evaluates clinical urgency against established cardiovascular and respiratory red-flag protocols to determine whether immediate emergency escalation is required. If emergency indicators are present, the system decides to initiate emergency responder dispatch and send immediate notifications to the designated family caregiver. In standard situations, the agent decides the appropriate medical specialist, selects an optimal consultation slot from hospital availability records, and coordinates accessible transit. Every decision and reasoning trajectory is surfaced directly to the patient and attending clinician through an interactive thought stream for verification.

## Inputs, Data Source, and Data Used
CuraLink AI relies strictly on verified medical data sources rather than unverified user assumptions. The primary data source consists of electronic health records containing longitudinal vitals, documented drug allergy arrays, active prescription lists, and laboratory panels formatted according to the HL7 FHIR R4 standard. Spoken voice input is captured and transcribed via the browser Web Speech API across six regional languages including English, Tamil, Hindi, Telugu, Kannada, and Malayalam. Additional input data used includes physician availability rosters, clinic room schedules, and wheelchair-accessible transportation options. All input telemetry is validated against patient identifiers before any operational workflow begins.

## Limits, Limitations, Constraints, and Known Issues
CuraLink AI operates within strict clinical limitation boundaries to prevent medical hallucinations and unauthorized actions. A critical limitation and constraint is that the agent cannot autonomously formulate medical diagnoses or modify pharmaceutical prescriptions without an attending doctor's explicit digital sign-off. Under the segregation of duties policy, the agent's role is restricted to proposing schedules and synthesizing clinical summaries, while final authorization remains constrained to licensed medical professionals. A known issue and operational limitation is the requirement for active network connectivity to confirm external transit reservations and dispatch caregiver notifications. When patient clinical records lack vital signs or data is older than sixty days, the agent enforces a constraint that alerts hospital administrative staff rather than inferring values.
