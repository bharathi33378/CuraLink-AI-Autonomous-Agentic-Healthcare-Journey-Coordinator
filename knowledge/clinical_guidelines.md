# Clinical Guidelines & Zero-Hallucination Standards

## Hypertension & Diabetes Management (AHA/ADA Standards)
* Target Blood Pressure for elderly patients: < 130/80 mmHg (customized per renal function).
* Fasting Blood Glucose targets: 80 - 130 mg/dL; HbA1c < 7.5% for active seniors, < 8.0% for frail seniors.

## Pharmacovigilance & Contraindication Matrix
* **ACE Inhibitors / ARBs (e.g. Telmisartan, Lisinopril)**: Contraindicated with severe bilateral renal artery stenosis; monitor for orthostatic hypotension.
* **Penicillins (e.g. Amoxicillin)**: Check patient allergy list. Never administer if documented allergy exists.
* **NSAIDs & Aspirin**: Evaluate GI bleeding risks when combined with anticoagulants or antiplatelet agents.
