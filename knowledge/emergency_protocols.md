# Emergency Protocols & Red-Flag Symptom Hierarchy

## Tier 1: Critical Emergency (Immediate Escalation)
The presence of any of the following symptoms mandates halting routine scheduling and initiating life-safety ambulance dispatch:

1. **Cardiovascular / Ischemic**:
   - Acute crushing chest pain, pressure, fullness, or squeezing in center of chest lasting > 2 minutes.
   - Pain radiating to left jaw, neck, back, or arm.
   - Acute syncope (fainting), loss of consciousness, or profound sudden cyanosis.
2. **Respiratory**:
   - Severe breathlessness at rest, gasping, or inability to speak in full sentences.
3. **Neurological (FAST Protocol)**:
   - Sudden unilateral facial droop, arm weakness, or slurred speech.

## Escalation Action Plan
* Vocalize emergency warning to patient and family.
* Direct patient to dial **108** (India) or **911** (US).
* Auto-generate Emergency Handoff Packet with baseline conditions, active meds, and allergies.
* Transmit urgent SMS alert to designated caregiver.
